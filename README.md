FACTLENS See the Facts. Verify the Truth. 

FACTLENS is a technology platform designed to help individuals and businesses research publicly available information, verify claims, organise evidence and produce reliable research reports.

Our Mission 

To make reliable information easier to find, assess and understand.

Core Features User registration and login. Research case management. Public-source information collection. Evidence and source-link organisation. Business and online seller verification. Fact-checking and claim assessment. Research report generation. User dashboard. Notifications and saved investigations. Technology 

The project will be developed progressively using accessible and affordable technologies.

Privacy and Responsible Research 

FACTLENS promotes lawful research and responsible information handling. It does not support stalking, doxxing, unauthorised access, harassment or exposure of private personal information.

Project Status
